## Insider buying clusters

Companies where **two or more insiders** reported buying shares **on the open market** in SEC Form 4 filings from the last 7 days (transaction code P). Grants, option exercises, purchases by 10% owners and pre-scheduled 10b5-1 plan trades are excluded; joint filers count once. **Sorted by total dollars reported.**

Context, not a forecast. finterm's own study of SEC filings from July 2025 to June 2026 looked at 1,404 open-market buys filed when a different insider of the same company had bought in the previous 30 days — related to this list, but a looser test (no dollar minimum, a longer window). Measured from the filing date, those stocks beat the S&P 500 (SPY) by a median 1.8% over the next month, 59% of the time; buys by a lone insider did nearly as well (median 1.2%, 55%). Over three months the median was −0.2%, a coin flip. Hypothetical, before costs and taxes, not adjusted for company size; past patterns do not predict future returns.
